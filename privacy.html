WrightTrack Privacy Policy

Effective November 13, 2026

WrightTrack is internal business software operated by Wright Steel LLC ("Wright Steel", "we", "us") of Harrison, Arkansas. It is used only by Wright Steel employees. This policy explains what information the software handles, where it is kept and who can see it.

1. Information the software handles

  • Business email sent to Wright Steel's sales mailbox, including attachments such as drawings, purchase orders and payment remittances.
  • Customer and vendor business contact details: company names, contact names, work email addresses, phone numbers and addresses.
  • Quotes, jobs, invoices and payments, including the related QuickBooks Online records (customers, vendors, estimates, invoices, sales receipts, purchase orders, payments and items) from Wright Steel's own QuickBooks company.
  • Employee sign-in details (name, work email, role) and a record of actions taken in the software.

The software does not collect information from the public, does not use advertising or tracking cookies, and does not handle consumer payment card numbers.

2. How the information is used

Only to run Wright Steel's business: sorting incoming requests, preparing and sending quotes, tracking jobs, pricing and ordering material, creating estimates, invoices, sales receipts and purchase orders in QuickBooks Online, matching payments to invoices, and keeping records of who approved what.

3. Where the information is kept

The software runs on a computer at the Wright Steel office in Harrison, Arkansas, which is encrypted and accessible only on the company network. Email and files stay in Wright Steel's Microsoft 365 account. Accounting records stay in Wright Steel's QuickBooks Online company.

4. Service providers

The software uses these providers, each under Wright Steel's own account and that provider's business terms:

  • Microsoft (Microsoft 365): email and SharePoint files.
  • Intuit (QuickBooks Online): accounting records. The software reads and writes only to QuickBooks companies owned by Wright Steel, after a Wright Steel administrator authorizes the connection, and a Wright Steel administrator can disconnect it at any time. Information received from Intuit is used only for Wright Steel's own accounting and is handled in line with Intuit's developer policies.
  • Anthropic (the Claude model): reads the text of emails, drawings and quote worksheets, together with the business records needed for the task in hand, to sort requests, draft quotes, vendor requests and purchase orders, read vendor prices and answer employees' questions about their work. Those records can include information that came from QuickBooks Online: customer and vendor names and business contact details, payment terms, item codes, and purchase order lines and prices. This is done through Anthropic's commercial API under its commercial terms, which do not allow Anthropic to train its models on this information. Anthropic receives it only to produce the result the software asked for.

We do not sell information and do not share it for marketing. Beyond the providers above, information is disclosed only when the law requires it.

Information from QuickBooks Online, in any form, is not used to train, fine-tune or develop any artificial intelligence or machine learning model, by Wright Steel or by any provider acting for it.

5. Access and security

Each employee has an individual sign-in and a role that limits what they can see and do. Nothing is sent to a customer or written to QuickBooks without a person approving it, and every approval and send is logged. Connection secrets for Microsoft 365, QuickBooks Online and Anthropic are stored on the office computer and are never shown back to users.

6. Retention

Quotes, jobs, invoices and the related correspondence are business records and are kept for as long as Wright Steel needs them for accounting, tax and warranty purposes.

7. Your choices

Customers and vendors who want to know what business contact information Wright Steel holds about them, or want it corrected, can write to jray@wrightsteelandmachine.com.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
