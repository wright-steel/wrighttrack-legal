WrightTrack

Internal operations software of Wright Steel LLC, Harrison, Arkansas

WrightTrack is an internal application used by Wright Steel LLC employees to manage requests for quote, quotes, open jobs, front-counter material sales and invoicing. It connects to the company's own Microsoft 365 and QuickBooks Online accounts.

WrightTrack is not offered to the public. It runs on a computer at the Wright Steel LLC office and can only be opened from the company's network by employees with a sign-in.
